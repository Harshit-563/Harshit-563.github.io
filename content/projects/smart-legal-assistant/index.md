Smart Legal Assistant is an NLP-powered contract analysis system for clause extraction, risk detection, and concise legal summarization.

<!--more-->

The project combines transformer-based language models with a lightweight FastAPI backend to analyze legal documents and highlight important clauses, risky terms, and key insights. It is designed to reduce the manual effort involved in contract review while keeping the application portable and easy to deploy with Docker.
