AutoScholar is an AI-powered research assistant that summarizes technical papers and generates practice questions to support faster learning and comprehension.

<!--more-->

The application uses transformer-based models for abstractive summarization and question generation, with a simple Streamlit interface for interactive use. It is designed to help users understand dense academic content more quickly without reading every section manually.
