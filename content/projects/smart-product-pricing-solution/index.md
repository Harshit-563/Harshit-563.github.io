A machine learning-based pricing prediction system using gradient boosting models to estimate optimal product prices from historical sales data.

<!--more-->

Smart Product Pricing Solution is a supervised regression system developed to predict optimal product prices using structured historical sales data. The project involved extensive exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, and transformation techniques including log normalization and categorical encoding. Multiple baseline models such as linear regression and random forest were evaluated before implementing gradient boosting models (LightGBM and XGBoost) for improved performance. The final pipeline included 5-fold cross-validation with early stopping to prevent overfitting, achieving a SMAPE score of 48.81 and RMSE of 0.658. The project demonstrates strong capabilities in tabular data modeling, ensemble learning, and performance optimization.
